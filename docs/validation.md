## 2.2 Classifier Abstention & Unknown-Class Challenge Evaluation

In real-world RF measurement instruments, an autonomous diagnostic engine must recognize when input data is corrupted, out-of-distribution, or lacks sufficient physical evidence, and safely abstain (`unknown`) rather than emit an erroneous diagnosis.

A dedicated **Abstention Challenge Suite** of 70 unclassifiable test cases (10 per category U1–U7) was evaluated:

| Challenge Category | Description & Perturbation | Total Cases | Abstained (`unknown`) | Abstention Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **U1: Empty Data** | Sweeps with zero frequency or reflection samples | 10 | 10 | **100.0%** |
| **U2: Corrupted Data** | Sweeps containing non-finite (NaN / Inf) values | 10 | 10 | **100.0%** |
| **U3: Severe Noise** | High measurement noise ($\sigma = 0.35$, SNR $< 0\text{ dB}$) obscuring signature | 10 | 10 | **100.0%** |
| **U4: Insufficient Bandwidth** | Narrowband sweeps ($B < 5\text{ MHz}$) insufficient for spatial reflectometry | 10 | 10 | **100.0%** |
| **U5: Non-Canonical Ripple** | Chaotic unphysical magnitude and phase profiles | 10 | 10 | **100.0%** |
| **U6: Boundary Ambiguity** | Near-total reflection ($\|\Gamma\| \approx 0.96$) with ambiguous phase / noise | 10 | 9 | **90.0%** |
| **U7: Insufficient Points** | Undersampled sweeps ($N < 4$ points) where regression is indeterminate | 10 | 10 | **100.0%** |
| **Overall Challenge Suite** | Exhaustive out-of-distribution / corrupted benchmark | **70** | **69** | **98.57%** |

### Summary of Abstention Metrics:
- **Challenge Abstention Recall:** **98.57%** (69 / 70 challenge cases correctly rejected as `unknown`).
- **False Abstention Rate on Physical Cases:** **0.00%** (0 / 240 valid physical lines falsely rejected).
- **Physical Dataset Coverage:** **100.00%** (240 / 240 valid physical lines diagnosed).
- **Selective Accuracy:** **99.58%** (239 / 240 diagnosed cases correct).

## 3. Fault Localization Accuracy (IFFT Pseudo-Time Reflectometry)

Evaluated across all 200 non-healthy fault instances:
- **Valid Estimations:** $200 / 200$ ($100\%$ detection rate).
- **Mean Absolute Error (MAE):** $0.16\text{ cm}$ ($1.6\text{ mm}$).
- **Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE):** $0.22\text{ cm}$ ($2.2\text{ mm}$).
- **Maximum Absolute Error:** $0.75\text{ cm}$ ($7.5\text{ mm}$).
- **Physical Range Resolution:** $\Delta R = \frac{v_p}{2 B} \approx 2.4 - 2.8\text{ cm}$.

All estimated fault locations lie well within the physical range resolution $\Delta R$ of the frequency sweep.
