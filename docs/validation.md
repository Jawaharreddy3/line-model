## 2. Statistical Diagnostic Classification Performance

Diagnostic validation was executed on a held-out test set generated by `validation/run_validation.py`:
- **Sample Size:** $N = 240$ randomized trials ($40$ trials per scenario across 6 canonical fault modes).
- **Line Parameters:** $Z_0 \in \{50\,\Omega, 75\,\Omega\}$, length $\in [0.5, 2.5]\text{ m}$, $\text{vf} \in \{0.66, 0.70, 1.0\}$.
- **Randomization:** Fault positions $d \in [0.15l, 0.85l]$, inductances $L \in [2, 40]\text{ nH}$, capacitances $C \in [2, 40]\text{ pF}$, mismatch loads $R_L \in [0.4Z_0, 2.5Z_0]$.
- **Measurement Noise:** Complex zero-mean Gaussian noise with $\sigma = 0.02$ on $S_{11}$.
- **Random Seed:** Documented fixed seed `42` for exact reproducibility.

> **Physical Analysis of Short Circuit Ambiguity:**
> For short circuits on lines where $l$ is an odd multiple of $\lambda/4$ at the lowest measurement frequency, the quarter-wave inversion causes the input impedance to look open at that frequency. The classifier conservatively identifies total reflection with positive low-frequency real reflection, leading to 18 cases classified as `open`. In real VNA measurements, a wider sweep down to DC ($f \to 0$) resolves this disambiguation.

## 3. Fault Localization Accuracy (IFFT Pseudo-Time Reflectometry)

Evaluated across all 200 non-healthy fault instances:
- **Valid Estimations:** $200 / 200$ ($100\%$ detection rate).
- **Mean Absolute Error (MAE):** $0.16\text{ cm}$ ($1.6\text{ mm}$).
- **Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE):** $0.22\text{ cm}$ ($2.2\text{ mm}$).
- **Maximum Absolute Error:** $0.75\text{ cm}$ ($7.5\text{ mm}$).
- **Physical Range Resolution:** $\Delta R = \frac{v_p}{2 B} \approx 2.4 - 2.8\text{ cm}$.

All estimated fault locations lie well within the physical range resolution $\Delta R$ of the frequency sweep.
